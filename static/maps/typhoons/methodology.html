Philippine Municipality–Storm Wind Exposure

Dataset period: 1998–2019   |   Map release: November 10, 2026   |   Status: Provisional modeled winds

Interpretation: These are model-generated estimates rather than direct local wind measurements. They have not undergone a complete independent meteorological validation. Some remote municipalities can have inflated estimates under the empirical outer-decay specification.

Panel construction

The master dataset contains exactly 860,683 municipality–storm observations: 529 individual cyclones times 1,627 fixed municipality/city geographic units. The key is the pair municipality_polygon_key + storm_id. No storm-year aggregation is used in the interactive display. Zero denotes modeled zero exposure, not proof of zero actual storm effects or disaster damage.

Sources and modeling

Cyclone paths and best-track identifiers draw on the International Best Track Archive for Climate Stewardship (IBTrACS), with Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) wind radii and intensity data used where available. A Holland-family inner profile, constrained by available JMA radii, is combined with an empirical outer-decay function. Population weighting uses historical WorldPop grids (approximately 100-m underlying population surfaces aggregated to approximately 1-km wind evaluation locations). Municipalities are represented by fixed polygons rather than shifting administrative names. The corrected release retains parameter and track-history quality flags.

Completeness and caveats

Of 860,683 rows, 826,860 have source histories marked complete. The remaining 33,823 have unresolved storm track history and are flagged on the map with orange dashed municipal outlines; their numeric values remain shown but may understate full-track exposure. The two five-year anomaly variables each have 215,378 missing values, mainly because complete prior-five-year histories are unavailable at the start of the series. The legacy 800-km reference has 2,029 missing values. All primary wind² cells are nonmissing.

The wind reconstruction is not a released CLIMADA/Holland (2008) rerun and should not be labeled as such. The current series is provisional pending validation against independent observations and alternative physical wind fields. It does not include observed rainfall or municipal disaster damages, nor should it be confused with the separately constructed IMERG rainfall datasets.

Technical provenance

The map displays the corrected master PH_wind_1998_2019.csv.gz (32 columns). For efficient loading, each map variable has been encoded as float32 in exact storm-major × municipality-major key order, then compressed to a separate binary file. Nonfinite values are retained as NaNs. The source-completeness flag is preserved as a separate binary array. The map loads each metric on demand; it does not replace the original high-precision CSV.
