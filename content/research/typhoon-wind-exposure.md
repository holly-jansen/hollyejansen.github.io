**Data and interpretation.** This is the corrected November 10, 2026 empirical outer-decay release, not the pending CLIMADA/Holland (2008) reconstruction. Winds are modeled, not observed municipal anemometer values; remote-location winds may be exaggerated by the empirical tail. For background, calculation methods, and data-quality limitations, see the methodology link below the map.
