        My research investigates the relationship between natural disasters, political accountability, gender, and public decision-making, primarily in the Philippines.

        ## Dissertation research

        ### Natural Disasters and the Electability of Women
        An examination of how typhoon exposure shapes candidate performance and electoral outcomes in Philippine mayoral elections.

        ### Strategic Storm Windows
        A study of the disaster-anticipation cycle and gendered legislative activity in the Philippine House of Representatives.

        ### The Response Trap
        A project on responsibility attribution, disaster spending, and the political incentives shaping response and prevention.
